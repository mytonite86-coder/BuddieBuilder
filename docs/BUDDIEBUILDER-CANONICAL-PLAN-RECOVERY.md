## CONFIRMED — recorded owner direction

- BuddieBuilder is the character creator/forge for a reusable offline learning-game ecosystem. The completed buddy should travel across later modules/levels, rather than being rebuilt in each game.
- Shared buddy data should retain selected parts, body/hair colors and name. Add only preferences justified by actual use. This does not authorize child accounts, cloud identity or a full profile-management platform.
- Family-first proving ground for Bjorn: offline, simple young-child operation, no advertising, subscriptions or unnecessary accounts/backend. Any clean commercial clone/adaptation is separately gated until shell, saves, controls and learning modules are proven.
- Preserve category progression: body → eyes → mouth → hair → arms → legs. Show live fitting; allow repeated swaps within a category; advance only through explicit Next. Keep the buddy large and visible.
- Hybrid tap/drag fitting maps choices to fixed renderer positions. Rejected drops preserve the buddy. Preserve the existing SVG renderer rather than introducing free placement, rotation, physics or a new geometry model.
- Simplify body/hair colors. Other parts should generally have intentional cartoon/natural colors. A friendly Monster Part Shelf was selected and implemented; do not reopen the clothesline/bowl/bin/shelf choice.
- Halloween/monster-first direction: zombie, skeleton, mummy, vampire, witch, monster. Friendly spooky, expressive, colorful and cartoonish; no gore, realistic horror, occult/satanic imagery, violence or disturbing body horror.
- Prototype SVG artwork is functional evidence, not final commercial art. Preserve it for interaction work; a later coherent art library remains possible.
- Dance, jump, wave, spin, laugh, sleep and excited are reusable character feedback/actions. They are not the complete future post-build game destination.

## Architecture and module boundaries

Immediate core: fixed-position SVG character, part/color state, tactile selection, existing animation actions and offline shell. Later saved buddy recipe supplies the minimum continuity contract for modules. The core character/profile should be consumed by games without taking over each game's rules.

Halloween is the first theme/content family, not a separate child idea and not evidence of completed monster packs. Learning rules can remain independent modules. Modules displaying the child's buddy need the shared recipe; their learning logic and content need not depend on a commerce service. Sound/haptic preferences are later proposals only when those features exist.

Parent/child separation: child-facing build/play remains direct and visual. Recovered proposals place installation/readiness, local-data explanation and eventual preferences/clear-all with grown-ups. Current implementation adds only a collapsible grown-up offline note. No parent account, gate, PIN or advanced color editor is decided or implemented.
